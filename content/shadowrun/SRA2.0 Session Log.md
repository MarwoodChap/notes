## Session Log

### Session — 22/9/26

Not long after ShadowPaw's ritual the city's power has come back on. ShadowPaw exited stage right, to visit her sister in Vancouver.

We watched a news broadcast where UCAS declared a state of emergency, and suspension of elections until 2050(?).

Stretch was contacted by Detective Riviera, who wanted us to try and find a missing girl, his cousin Samantha. - offering ¥4.5k She's been missing for a month or so. We agreed to meet him at 10:00 at the Chicago's Own Pizzeria, in Cabbage Town. Beckett did some digging into Riviera. First name Nicholas. Recently promoted to detective. Has a rep as a white app.

Sat with his back to the door we entering, like a gonk, unaware of us entering. We startled him as we came up to him.

Samantha was last see on North Green View Avenue, an old industrial estate in 
Not sure what she was doing there/ She's a human white woman, early 20s, with short black hair. She looks like she's walking with a purpose in a photo we're given. A friend of hers said she was being paid to be a lab rat by an outfit called Janus Industries. He seemed worried about being overheard as he said this. A housemate had said she had been having strange dreams. No partner or SO. Bit of an otaku, but not sure if she's a technomancer. Address 155 Baker Street, Cabbage Town. We have keys. She has had a housemate, but she went back to Québec when the sectarian problems kicked off.

We kicked off some planning:
Beckett: 3 (Contacts and Network)
Stretch: 3 (Electronic research)
BrainJack: 4 (Astral surveillance)
Total:10

We then went to her apartment, which is on an upper floor, half way down a corridor. The area is pretty low-rent, and studenty
Her front door wasn't forced, but had definetly been kicked, at about shin height.
We were interrupted by a neighbour, an old ork woman. Said some people came and took her away - in natty suits, about 6 weeks ago. Prior to that, someone (male) tried to come and kick her door, but she wouldn't let them in. Smart girl, scientist. Oh, and she had a translucent spiral on her shoulder that followed her around. (Sounds like a sprite, a new kind of spirit found in the matrix, possibly like a familiar for a technomancer)

We went in, and BrainJack cleverly worked out the lights weren't trapped, by turning them on. The place looked like it had been systematically searched - not by her. It hadn't been turned over straight away after her disappearance. Also, they whole place was trashed, suggested they hadn't found what they were looking for. Finding nothing else, and setting up an agreement with Victoria to call us if she sees anything we decided to leave.

As we left, Brainjack spotted small Robin-sized drone which zipped off as soon as we appeared.
