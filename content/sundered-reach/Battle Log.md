## Chapter One

### 001 — The Bloodying of Cerelia

**Date:** 26.08.2026 · **Combatants:** [[./The Red Knights of Oblivion|The Red Knights of Oblivion]] vs [[./House Crosius|House Crosius]] · **World:** [[Cerelia|Cerelia]] · **Result:** World Eaters victory, 42–25

> Crosius household troops held prepared positions on the grain plains and were overrun inside a day. The Crosius right flank collapsed under Khârn and his Berzerkers; an incendiary tank engaged Prince Verux directly and achieved nothing at all. The line did not break so much as get eaten, end to end, the last section dying on the final action of the day. In the eastern trenches the Red Knights turned up something sealed, which they have since bolted to a war engine and stopped thinking about.

**Changed:** Red Knights of Oblivion recover **Component I**. Crosius learns, belatedly, that a survey detail was working those trenches under Ordo Scriptorum authority that the Ordo Scriptorum did not issue.

**Full account:** [[./2026-08-26 The Bloodying of Cerelia (DRAFT)|2026-08-26 The Bloodying of Cerelia (DRAFT)]]

### 002 — The Listening of Auspicia

**Date:** 13.09.2026 · **Combatants:** [[./House Crosius|House Crosius]] vs [[./The Aeldari of the Sundered Reach|The Aeldari of the Sundered Reach]] · **World:** [[./The Ten|Auspicia]] · **Result:** Crosius victory, 60–47

> Both forces made for an ancient long-range auspex array — four outstations on a fixed baseline, one central cogitator hall — and neither was there to fight. A section of the 23rd took the hall in the first hour and held it down to the sergeant before the bullgryns arrived and proved unkillable. On the right, Tempestus Aquilons were dropped into the Harlequins' path and annihilated to a man, buying two hours the flank could not have survived without. Mortar and malleus fire brought down Farseer Aethenor in the fourth hour; Iyanden did not break, but stopped, and left in good order with two of the five stations.

**Changed:** Crosius holds the hall and recovers the array's cogitator core — corrupt, partial, and sealed straight into Ordo Scriptorum custody. Iyanden leaves with Aethenor's reading of the baseline. Neither side can act on what it has. The core's seals had been cut and reset **before** the battle, and one partition wiped with precision. Lord Martial Honoria Calvert takes direct field command from here on.

**Full account:** [[./2026-09-13  The Listening of Auspicia (DRAFT)|2026-09-13  The Listening of Auspicia (DRAFT)]]

### 004 — The First Battle of Vessant

**Date:** 28.10.2026 · **Combatants:** [[./The Discordant Duet|The Discordant Duet]] vs [[./The Aeldari of the Sundered Reach|The Aeldari of the Sundered Reach]] · **World:** [[./The Ten|Vessant]] · **Result:** Emperor's Children victory, 38–20

> Kaelen and Sylas came to the ruined churches of Vessant hunting a relic; Aethenor came to watch them, and was fed decoys for his trouble. Malakor's Noise Marines silenced the Dark Reapers and later the Fire Dragons, while the Harlequins and a Solitaire cut down the Flawless Blades on the far flank. Kaelen fell to Aeldari precision fire and was carried from the field by his Tormentors; no body has been found. Sylas's Infractors, meanwhile, sat out most of the battle guarding what they had found.

**Changed:** The Discordant Duet recover **Component II**. The site had been opened and closed again before either force arrived — whoever got there first left the component where it lay. Kaelen has fallen; his fate is unconfirmed. Slaanesh has noticed the performance: the Duet take 150 points of daemonic reinforcements for one future game.

**Full account:** [[./The First Battle of Vessant|The First Battle of Vessant]]
