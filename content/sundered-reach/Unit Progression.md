# The Sundered Reach — Unit Progression

A stripped-down Crusade system for the campaign: no Supply Limit, no Requisition Points, no Order of Battle roster management. Just units — and named characters — getting better at what they already do when they do something worth remembering.

## How it works

**Earning XP** A unit earns XP after any battle it fought in:

| **Trigger**                                                     | **XP**  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | --- |
| Fought in the battle                                        | +1  |
| Destroyed 3+ enemy units                                    | +1  |
| Survived without being destroyed                            | +1  |
| Ended the battle at full strength (not Below Half-strength) | +1  |
| Completed a personal Agenda (a goal set before the game)    | +1  |
| Did something narratively excellent (GM/group call)         | +1  |

**Ranking up**

| **Rank**            | **XP threshold** | **Honours available** |
| --------------- | ------------ | ----------------- |
| Battle-ready    | 0            | —                 |
| Blooded         | 6            | 1                 |
| Battle-hardened | 16           | 2                 |
| Heroic          | 31           | 3                 |
| Legendary       | 51           | 4                 |

Each time a unit crosses a threshold, it gains a **Battle Honour**.

**Battle Honours** Pick one per rank-up:

- **Battle Trait** — a bolt-on special rule (generic Crusade tables or faction-specific, from [Wahapedia's 10th-Edition Crusade pages](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k10ed/the-rules/crusade-rules/#Ranks-and-Battle-Honours))
- **Weapon Modification** — a permanent upgrade to one of the unit's weapons
- **Relic** — Characters only, unique wargear

No Battle Scars — this tracker only rewards, it doesn't punish bad rolls.

**Named characters and Crucible of Champions** Characters built using the Crucible of Champions rules (custom archetype + specialism datasheets) plug into the same XP/Rank/Honour loop as any other unit — Crucible characters just draw their Honours from the Character-only pool (Battle Traits and Relics; no Weapon Modifications unless their build allows it). Crucible doesn't include its own progression system, so this tracker is what gives characters like Crosius, Thule, and later arrivals a way to visibly grow across the campaign rather than staying static from their first appearance.
