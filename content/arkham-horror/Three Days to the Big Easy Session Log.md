## The Investigators

### Jim Culver (Kate)
Jazz trumpeter, booked to play the *Gulf Queen*'s saloon. Carries his late father's trumpet, the one with the strange writing inside the bell. Cautious to a fault.

### Alessandra Zorzi (JP)
Self-styled countess and professional con artist, aboard for the casino. Recognised by the Outfit's man on board almost immediately.

### Dexter Drake (Andy)
Stage magician who has seen real magic and wants more of it. Hired to entertain the passengers.

## Session Log

### Session 1 — 12/10/26

The *Gulf Queen*, a luxury paddle steamer, left Greenville on Saturday morning, bound for New Orleans in time for Mardi Gras. Once out on the river the boat revealed itself as a floating casino and speakeasy, with the bottles appearing from behind the bar and the tables turning into card games, roulette and craps.

On Saturday night a cry of *"Man overboard!"* stopped the music. The body pulled from the river was Dante "Dane" Russo, a loud young Chicago hoodlum who had spent the day drinking, losing at poker and insulting the staff. He had been shot in the back at close range. Johnny Greco, the Outfit's representative on board, quietly recruited the investigators to find the killer before the boat reached New Orleans, on the reasonable grounds that Russo's father, Vinnie "the Knife" Russo, would otherwise start working through the passenger list himself.

The trail led quickly to Leon Phillips, a steward everyone described as the kindest man on the crew, sitting on his bunk holding Russo's pistol and calmly admitting the murder. He'd picked up a cigarette lighter Russo dropped, followed him out to return it, and then simply… shot him.

Alessandra pocketed the lighter, a mother-of-pearl piece carved with a German eagle. It didn't stay pocketed. It vanished and turned up among Russo's belongings in the captain's keeping.

On Sunday morning the boat struck a sandbar. Minutes later Captain Dubois threw her helmsman through the pilot house window, killing him, and calmly explained that he'd deserved it. That afternoon two of Greco's own people, Jerry James and Donna Draghetti, picked a fight with the investigators in the Grand Saloon over nothing at all, and died for it at the hands of Greco and his remaining men.

By now the investigators had seen the pattern: wherever the lighter went, murder followed. Greco had it. Alessandra lifted it from his pocket, leaving behind a convincing copy that Drake had made for the purpose. They destroyed it before Greco, by then raging at everyone, could do whatever he was working himself up to.

### Session 2 — 21/10/26

As the investigators staggered back to their cabins, the Russian émigré Baron Kuznetsov, very drunk, seized on Drake, shouting *"Колдун!"* ("sorcerer!") and begging him to bring his dead wife back. Drake shrugged him off. The Baron drank himself into a stupor, and Alessandra relieved him of his valuables.

In the aftermath of the lighter's destruction, Jim felt an urge to check his trumpet. A pale blue flame was playing around the bell. When it faded, a new name was scratched into the brass alongside his father's: **Patrick Finnigan**. A silver dollar lay in the trumpet case, and a smiling, impeccably polite stranger introduced himself as **Mr Smith**. Jim wanted nothing to do with him. Drake pocketed the coin as a souvenir.

Alessandra went for the ship's safe and found the gambler Layla Huang already working the lock. Rather than fight over it, the two of them emptied it together, and Layla handed Alessandra her share: $1,000.

Greco, meanwhile, had pulled himself together enough to warn the investigators that the new first mate, Harrigan, was a Prohibition agent. He had the liquor dumped over the side and the gambling tables hidden before the *Gulf Queen* reached New Orleans.

The raid came anyway. The police were after two things, the booze and a bribe, and found neither: the booze was in the Mississippi and the bribe money was gone from the safe. They beat Greco and hauled him off in handcuffs, then let everyone else go. Phillips and Captain Dubois were left aboard.

The investigators had almost made it ashore when they were stopped by Zizzi, Vinnie "the Knife" Russo's caporegime, and marched back aboard for questioning. They laid everything at the door of the dead helmsman, Tanner Sinclair, and Zizzi believed every word. They were allowed to slip away, and Jim and Drake were even offered work on the return journey, should they want it.

That's where we left them: ashore in New Orleans on the eve of Mardi Gras.
